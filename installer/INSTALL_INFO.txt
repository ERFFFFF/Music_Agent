Music Agent - Spotify Playback Controller

Before installing, make sure you have:

1. A Spotify account (free or premium)
2. A Spotify Developer app created at:
   https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard/

3. Your Client ID and Client Secret ready
   (found in your Spotify app's Settings page)

The installer will ask for these credentials and verify
them with Spotify before completing the installation.

Note: On first launch, a browser window will open once for
Spotify authorization. After that, the app runs silently in
the background and responds to keyboard shortcuts.
