Music Agent - Playback Controller

This installer only copies files. You choose how to control your
music the first time you launch the app:

  Cadence account
     Sign in to your Cadence server (the same account you use in
     the web player). Nothing else to set up, and it works on a
     network where Spotify itself is blocked.
     Keep a Cadence tab open in your browser - it is what performs
     the commands.

  Spotify app
     The app asks for a Spotify Client ID and Client Secret, which
     you create at https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard/ , and
     saves them to a file for you. On first use a browser window
     opens once for Spotify authorization.

Either way the app then runs silently in the background and
responds to your keyboard shortcuts. You can switch accounts later
from the tray icon: right-click - Settings.
